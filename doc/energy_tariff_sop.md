# Sample Energy Tariff and SOP (demo only, replace with real documents)

## Electricity tariff
- Peak hours (14:00-21:00): $0.18 per kWh
- Standard hours (06:00-14:00 and 21:00-22:00): $0.11 per kWh
- Off-peak hours (22:00-06:00): $0.07 per kWh
- Demand charge: $12 per kW, based on the highest demand of the month.

## Peak demand SOP
- Peak shaving target: reduce demand in peak hours by about 12 percent.
- Move about half of the reduced load to off-peak hours (01:00-06:00).
- If the forecast peak is above 90% of contracted capacity, the energy manager must be notified 24 hours before.
- Flexible loads must be moved out of peak hours. Flexible loads include batch processing, EV charging, water pumping and HVAC pre-cooling.
- Critical loads (servers, safety systems, cold storage) must never be shifted.

## Weekend and low-demand days
- On days where forecast demand is more than 10% below the monthly average, schedule maintenance and testing of equipment.
- Run heavy equipment in off-peak hours (22:00-06:00) whenever possible.

## Reporting
- The energy manager reviews the 10-day forecast every Monday and records any load-shifting decisions.
